About Me

Tech-enthusiast DevOps Engineer from Kurunegala, Sri Lanka

Born in 1999, I began my primary and secondary education at Maliyadeva College in 2005. After completing the GCE Advanced Level examination in 2018, I enrolled at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT) to pursue higher education in Information Technology. After graduating, I started my career at iTelaSoft, where I gained industry experience in DevOps and cloud technologies. I currently work as a Senior DevOps Engineer at Sysco LABS Sri Lanka, focusing on building scalable cloud platforms, automation, and improving system reliability using modern DevOps practices.
